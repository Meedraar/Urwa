Our Learning Programmes

Whether you are beginning your Qur’an journey, improving your recitation or working towards memorisation, there is room for you to learn, grow and progress at URWA.

01 QUR’AN RECITATION

Qur’an Halaqa

Join a supportive learning circle where sisters strengthen their Qur’an recitation, practise regularly and grow together in their relationship with the Book of Allah.

02 RECITATION MASTERY

Tajweed

Learn the rules of Tajweed and apply them in your recitation. Develop a stronger understanding of articulation points, characteristics of letters and proper pronunciation.

03 QUR’ANIC UNDERSTANDING

Tafseer

Explore the meanings, context and lessons of the Qur’an. Reflect on its guidance and discover ways to bring its teachings into your everyday life.

Hifdh Hub
