Our Community

URWA is more than a place to attend lessons. It is a community of learners, educators and volunteers who support one another in seeking knowledge and serving the Book of Allah.

A shared journey

Many roles, one purpose

Every member contributes to a learning environment built on companionship, service and a shared commitment to the Qur’an.

Our learners

Sisters and brothers come together in halaqahs to recite, memorise, revise and deepen their understanding of the Qur’an.

Dedicated Qur’an educators guide students through their learning and help nurture consistency, understanding and growth.
