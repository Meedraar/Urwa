The story behind our beginning

URWA began with five sisters who wanted the Qur’an to stay part of everyday life, not only Ramadan. In Shawwal 1442, twelve sisters gathered for the first halaqah. Brothers’ halaqahs were established in 1446 AH, and now include several halaqahs. By Allah’s permission, that beginning grew into a wider family connected by the Book of Allah.

Our milestones

Our journey through the years

Every milestone represents the dedication, growth and efforts of the people who have helped build this community.

2021

Our first halaqah began with twelve sisters.

2022

Our community grew to approximately fifty members.

2023–2024

Day of Arafah, Eid celebrations, Memorise and Rise, and December retreats.

2025

Structured learning levels, teams and brothers’ halaqahs.

2026

Our community grew to over six hundred members.
