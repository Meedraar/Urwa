The Teams Behind URWA

Meet the six departments and the responsibilities they carry.

Halaqa Manager Team

Oversees the smooth running of URWA halaqahs and supports teachers and students in their learning journey.

Key responsibilities

  • Coordinate halaqah schedules and class arrangements.
  • Monitor attendance and student participation.
  • Support Ablas and address day-to-day class challenges.
  • Follow up on student progress and learning needs.

Finance Team

Manages URWA’s financial resources and records with honesty, transparency and responsibility.

Key responsibilities

  • Prepare budgets for programmes, events and operations.
  • Record income, expenses and contributions.
  • Maintain receipts and supporting documents.
  • Provide financial updates to authorised leadership.

Social Media Team

Looks after URWA’s online presence and shares programme updates, announcements and beneficial reminders.

Key responsibilities

  • Plan content and maintain a publishing calendar.
  • Create posters, captions and videos.
  • Promote classes, events and community activities.
  • Review engagement and communicate audience feedback.

Event Team

Plans and coordinates URWA events and gatherings, making each activity purposeful and welcoming.

Key responsibilities

  • Plan event schedules and programme activities.
  • Coordinate venues, budgets, logistics and volunteers.
  • Work with the Social Media Team on publicity.
  • Manage registration and participant communication.

Recruitment Team

Welcomes volunteers and team members through an organised and transparent recruitment process.

Key responsibilities

  • Share approved volunteer opportunities.
  • Coordinate applications and selection.
  • Work with departments to identify team needs.
  • Support onboarding and role orientation.

Student Affairs & Welfare Team

Supports students, listens to their concerns and helps them feel respected throughout their URWA learning journey.

Key responsibilities

  • Receive and follow up student concerns.
  • Help resolve routine student matters.
  • Support students facing learning or participation challenges.
  • Escalate serious or unresolved matters to Administration.
  • Handle student information confidentially.
Every department contributes to one shared purpose: helping our community grow closer to the Qur’an.
