Our story

From a small circle to a growing Qur’an family.

URWA began with five sisters who wanted the Qur’an to stay part of everyday life, not only Ramadan. In Shawwal 1442, twelve sisters gathered for the first halaqah. Brothers’ halaqahs were established in 1446 AH, and now include several halaqahs. By Allah’s permission, that beginning grew into a wider family connected by the Book of Allah.

Our mission, vision and moral compass.

لَقَدْ جَاءَكُمْ رَسُولٌ مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ عَزِيزٌ عَلَيْهِ مَا عَنِتُّمْ حَرِيصٌ عَلَيْكُم بِالْمُؤْمِنِينَ رَءُوفٌ رَّحِيمٌ
Surah At-Tawbah, 9:128

Our vision

Creating meaningful connections with the Quran and its people, fostering growth and empowering a community that lives by its teachings.

Our mission

To have the Quran recited in every Muslim home in Kenya, touching homes around the globe transforming them into stars on earth.

Our Moral compass

من أنفسكم
Belonging

No matter where you come from, your colour, race, background or circumstances, you have a place in URWA.

عزيز عليه ما عنتم
Empathy

We refuse to become indifferent to the struggles of others.

حريص عليكم
Concern

We care about what becomes of one another in this life and the Hereafter.

رؤوف
Kindness

We value strength tempered with gentleness, correction accompanied by wisdom and leadership grounded in consideration.

رحيم
Compassion

We uphold patience over harshness, understanding over condemnation and forgiveness over resentment.
